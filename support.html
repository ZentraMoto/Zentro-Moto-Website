Support & FAQ

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, your bike and warranty — in one place.

Ordering Delivery & Collection Your Bike Warranty & Parts Returns

Ordering

What does IN STOCK mean?
The bike is physically available at Zentro Moto in Newcastle and ready to purchase now.
What does INCOMING mean?
The bike is confirmed and on its way to Zentro Moto. You can reserve one ahead of arrival.
What does AVAILABLE TO ORDER mean?
The bike can be ordered in. Zentro Moto will confirm sourcing and an estimated lead time with you.
How do reservations work?
A reservation secures your bike ahead of arrival. See Reservation / Order Terms for deposit, refund and lead-time details.

Delivery & Collection

Newcastle collection
Collect your bike directly from Zentro Moto in Newcastle, NSW. See Shipping & Collection for details.
Delivery areas
Freight can be arranged to eligible Australian locations. See Shipping & Collection.
Delivery timing
Timing depends on the bike’s availability status and your location. Zentro Moto will confirm an estimate with you directly.

Your Bike

What is included?
Each bike is supplied with its battery, charger and documentation, plus any included factory accessories for that model.
Setup / assembly
Whether assembly is required is noted on each bike’s product page.
Documentation
Documentation is included with every bike.

Warranty & Parts

12-Month Factory Warranty
The bike is supplied with a 12-month factory warranty covering core powertrain components: battery, controller, motor and frame, subject to the applicable warranty terms and exclusions.
How to make a warranty claim
See Warranty & Returns for the full claims process, including what information to provide.
Genuine OEM replacement parts
Zentro Moto supplies genuine OEM replacement parts for supported models.
Wear-and-tear items
Common wear-and-tear items are available through Zentro Moto.
Selected performance upgrades
A selection of performance upgrades is available — contact Zentro Moto for current options.
Australian Consumer Law rights
Any factory warranty is in addition to rights that may apply under the Australian Consumer Law. Zentro Moto remains the customer’s first point of contact for products purchased from Zentro Moto.

Returns

Faulty products
Contact Zentro Moto as soon as possible if you believe your bike has a fault. See Warranty & Returns.
Returns / cancellations
See Warranty & Returns for the current returns and cancellation process.
Change-of-mind policy
Any change-of-mind policy offered by Zentro Moto is set out in Warranty & Returns.
