Full-size power. Precise control.

The Ultra Bee brings Surron's electric performance into a larger, more capable off-road platform, built for riders wanting stronger performance, greater stability and serious off-road capability.

Key features

Performance highlights.

01

24.5 kW Next-Generation Power

Hairpin motor system delivering up to 24.5 kW of peak power and 520 Nm of torque.

02

74V / 60Ah High-Performance Battery

Removable 74V / 60Ah battery with up to 115 km of manufacturer-claimed range at 50 km/h.

03

Advanced Riding Modes & Traction Control

Six riding modes with adjustable traction control, regenerative braking and throttle response.

04

Performance Suspension & Brakes

KKE suspension with 240 mm of front and rear travel, paired with four-piston hydraulic brakes.

05

Connected Rider Technology

App connectivity for status monitoring, GPS, parameter adjustment and over-the-air updates.
