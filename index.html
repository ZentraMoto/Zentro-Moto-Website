Frequently asked questions

Are the bikes genuine Surrons?
Yes. Zentro Moto supplies genuine Surron motorcycles sourced directly from Surron in Chongqing, China. Bikes have VIN identification, documented specifications and the required battery/DG documentation, giving customers clear traceability of what they are buying.
What warranty and support is included?
Bikes are supplied with a 12-month factory warranty covering core components including the battery, controller, motor and frame, subject to the applicable warranty terms and exclusions. Zentro Moto provides direct after-sales support and can assist with sourcing genuine replacement parts where required.
Do you deliver Australia-wide?
Yes. Zentro Moto ships bikes securely in crates to eligible locations across Australia. Freight pricing and delivery details are provided during the ordering process.
Does the bike arrive assembled?
Bikes arrive approximately 90% assembled from Surron. Final assembly is typically limited to items such as fitting the handlebars, attaching the front wheel and installing the fender. Assembly requirements can vary slightly by model.
How does Zentro Moto keep prices lower than traditional dealerships?
Zentro Moto operates with a lean, low-overhead model. Rather than carrying large volumes of local stock and the cost base of a traditional dealership, customer orders are consolidated into supplier batches. This helps reduce overheads and allows us to pass more of the savings directly on to customers while still providing genuine Surron bikes, a 12-month factory warranty on core components, after-sales support and Australia-wide delivery.
