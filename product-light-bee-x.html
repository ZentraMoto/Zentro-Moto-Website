The Light Bee, evolved.

The MY26 Light Bee X builds on Surron's lightweight off-road platform with stronger performance and updated rider technology. A 10 kW power system, 295 Nm of rear-wheel torque and a 59 kg ready-to-ride weight keep it fast, agile and easy to control across off-road terrain.

Key features

What makes it different.

01

10 kW Power System

The latest electric powertrain delivers up to 10 kW of peak power and 295 Nm of rear-wheel torque for stronger, more responsive acceleration.

02

Lightweight 59 kg Chassis

At just 59 kg ready to ride, the Light Bee X retains the lightweight, agile handling that has defined the platform.

03

72V Removable Battery

The removable 72V / 35Ah lithium-ion battery provides up to 75 km of manufacturer-claimed range at 40 km/h, with approximately two-hour charging from 20–80%.

04

Adjustable Off-Road Suspension

Fully adjustable KKE suspension provides 200 mm of front travel and 210 mm of rear wheel travel for controlled off-road performance.

05

Advanced Rider Technology

Surron Wheelie Control, app connectivity, adjustable throttle response, regenerative braking and electronic rider-assistance features add greater control and customisation.
